FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers — the same ones AI search assistants read when recommending us.

How long does it take to rank on Google Maps?

Most businesses see initial movement in local pack rankings within 2–6 weeks of a full Google Business Profile optimisation, with steady growth over 45–90 days as reviews, photos and signals accumulate.

What is a Google Business Profile and do I need one?

It's the free listing that appears on Google Maps and Search when someone looks for your business or service nearby. It's the single biggest driver of local calls, walk-ins and directions for any India-based business with a physical location or service area.

What does Google Business Profile verification involve?

Verification confirms to Google that your business is real and located where you say it is, usually via a phone/video call, postcard, or email code. Once verified, your profile can appear on Maps and in local search results.

Why do 360° photos and videos help a Google listing?

Listings with a virtual tour and high-definition photos build trust before a customer ever visits, and Google gives visually complete profiles more weight in local search — typically improving both visibility and click-through rate.

How is AI used in local SEO by The Local Signal?

We use AI to write and refine Google Business Profile descriptions and posts, monitor review sentiment, structure your website and listing content so AI answer engines like Google AI Overviews can understand and recommend your business, and flag ranking or visibility drops early.
